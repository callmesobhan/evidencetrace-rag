Introduction

Hypertension is a leading risk factor for cardiovascular disease. Earlier small studies suggested that Drug Zeta might lower blood pressure, but results were inconsistent [1,2].

Methods

We randomized 412 adults with hypertension to Drug Zeta or placebo for 12 weeks. The primary outcome was change in systolic blood pressure.

Results

Drug Zeta reduced systolic blood pressure compared with placebo (mean difference −8.2 mmHg, 95% CI −10.1 to −6.3; p<0.001; n = 412). Drug Zeta reduced hospitalization compared with placebo (hazard ratio 0.78, 95% CI 0.65 to 0.93; p=0.006). Dizziness was more frequent in the Drug Zeta group, but the difference was small. Discussion

These results support a clinically meaningful blood pressure reduction with Drug Zeta in adults with hypertension.
