Zeta-Community: Drug Zeta in a Community Hypertension Programme
[SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - fictional drug and fictional data]

Abstract

Drug Zeta reduced systolic blood pressure compared with placebo in a community programme.

Methods

We enrolled 230 adults from 14 community clinics and randomized them to Drug Zeta or placebo for 12 weeks.

Results

Drug Zeta reduced systolic blood pressure compared with placebo (mean difference −6.0 mmHg, 95% CI −9.0 to −3.0; p<0.001; n = 230).

Discussion

Results were consistent across clinics.
