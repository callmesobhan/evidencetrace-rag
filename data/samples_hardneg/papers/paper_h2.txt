Abstract

A fictional drug and its placebo arm.

Methods

We randomized adults to the study drugs or placebo and followed them for 12 months.

Results

Drug Sigma did not change stroke risk, whereas placebo increased myocardial infarction (hazard ratio 1.40, 95% CI 1.10 to 1.78; p=0.005). Drug Sigma reduced cardiovascular death (hazard ratio 0.80, 95% CI 0.66 to 0.97; p=0.02).
