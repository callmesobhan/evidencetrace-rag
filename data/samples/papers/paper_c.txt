Zeta-Pilot: A Small Pilot Trial of Drug Zeta for Blood Pressure
[SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - fictional drug and fictional data]

Abstract

In a pilot trial of 96 adults, Drug Zeta did not significantly reduce systolic blood pressure compared with placebo.

Methods

Ninety-six adults with mild hypertension were randomized to Drug Zeta or placebo for 8 weeks.

Results

Drug Zeta did not significantly reduce systolic blood pressure compared with placebo (mean difference −2.1 mmHg, 95% CI −5.0 to 0.8; p=0.15; n = 96).

Discussion

The pilot was not powered to detect small effects.
