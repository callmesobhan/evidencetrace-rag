THE RULES
  Scope rules v1.2 (adopted for this re-judgement; see the annotation guidelines, v1.2
  addendum). This form re-judges 14 items from two papers (PMC12703296 and PMC12117457). The two
  adjudicators applied two scope rules differently on these papers, so the rules are now written
  down. Judge each item under the rules as written here, not under your own preferred rule.

  Rule 1, per-unit estimates. An estimate given per one unit of a continuous variable, from a
  regression model, is in scope only if that variable is the primary exposure the paper is about
  (for example the vasopressor dose NEE in a study of kidney replacement therapy). Per-unit
  estimates for covariates that the model adjusts for (for example age, weight or mean arterial
  pressure) are out of scope, whatever scale label the paper uses (MD, OR, HR). A categorised
  exposure compared with a reference level is in scope.

  Rule 2, contrasts between conditions. A contrast between two named conditions or exposure
  levels reported in the same paper (for example high versus low altitude in a crossover trial)
  is in scope as a mean difference when the text states it as a difference between those
  conditions, including "higher by" or "lower by" wording, even if a table labels it "mean
  change". A change from baseline within one condition is out of scope.

  For each item answer yes (a genuine in-scope effect estimate under these rules), no, or
  unsure. A short note is useful wherever you applied a rule. This is a fresh judgement: do not
  copy your earlier verdicts.

  In general, an item is in scope if it is a between-group point estimate (HR, OR, RR, IRR, MD,
  SMD, RD; optionally with CI and p). It is not in scope if it is a planned or hypothetical
  value, a baseline-balance statistic, a within-arm change, a rate or proportion, a regression
  coefficient, or an unnamed difference. You are judging the estimate, not who found it.
  The full guidelines:
  https://github.com/callmesobhan/evidencetrace-rag/blob/main/docs/annotation-guidelines.md (the
  v1.2 addendum is at the end).
