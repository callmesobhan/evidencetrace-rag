Adjudication: who is right, the system or the annotator?

For each item, decide whether it is a genuine effect estimate of the kind the annotation guidelines define: a between-group point estimate (HR, OR, RR, IRR, MD, SMD, RD; optionally with CI and p), not a planned/hypothetical value, a baseline-balance statistic, a within-arm change, a rate or proportion, a regression coefficient, or an unnamed difference. You are judging the estimate, not who found it. Keys: y in scope, n not in scope, u unsure, j/k next/previous.
