# Attribution

The annotation, gold, prediction and report files in `data/m1/` quote sentences (about 200,000 characters in total,
mostly in `annotation/*_candidates.jsonl` and `*_nearmiss.jsonl`) from the 166 open-access articles below. Each is
published in PubMed Central under **CC BY** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): the licence of every article
was read from its own JATS metadata when it was downloaded, and only CC BY / CC0 articles were kept.

**Changes made to the quoted text:** passages are excerpted (single sentences or short spans), Unicode is normalised
(NFKC; hyphen variants mapped to "-"; line breaks and a few control characters folded; Lancet-style middle-dot decimals
`0·78` read as `0.78`), and tables, figures and formulae are dropped before sentences are cut. Nothing is paraphrased.
The articles themselves are not redistributed: follow the link in each row to the original.

which remain under their authors' CC BY terms.
